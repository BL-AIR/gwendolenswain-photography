About

Hello, I’m Gwendolen.

Photographer — Melbourne

I’m a Melbourne photographer, and I hold a Bachelor of Arts (Photography) from RMIT.

My training is in art photography — making bodies of work, thinking hard about light, and paying attention to people and the spaces they move through. Most of the photographs on this site come from that study.

I bring the same approach to freelance work. At an event, I keep to the edges of the room so people stay themselves. In a portrait sitting, I take my time, so you can forget the camera is there. And when an artist or organisation needs their work documented, I treat it with the care I’d want for my own.

If you have something in mind, I’d be glad to hear about it.

Working Together

Simple from first message to final gallery.

1. Tell me about it

Send me the date, the place and what matters most to you. I’ll come back with my availability and a quote.

2. On the day

I arrive early, learn the space and keep out of the way. You get on with your event — I’ll look after the pictures.

3. Your photographs

Every image is edited by hand and delivered in an online gallery, ready to download and share.
